## Accessibility (§7) — what was checked

- **Keyboard:** every screen can be used with Tab / Enter / Space / Esc. In a game, cards are focusable; arrow keys move between them (current choices first), Enter acts on the focused card, and focusing a card shows its full-size preview. Verified by playing a Battle Unit with the keyboard alone.
- **Screen readers:** an automated pass over the title, main menu, settings, local setup, deck builder, How to Play, rules reference, developer menu, card editor and game screen found no control without an accessible name and no image without alt text. Cards announce name, type, strengths, text, state (available / can't be played now and why / target / selected / depleted / disoriented). Live regions cover prompts, toasts and the log.
- **Text size** (90–130%) scales all interface text; checked at 130% with no clipped text in the game's side panel. **High contrast** and **colourblind-safe mode** (shape cues on ability and Creation Unit colours) are settings.
- **Touch:** cards can be dragged with a finger (no page scrolling or zooming in the way); tap works as click.

## Still to check on your side

1. **Two computers, two networks (item 7).** Either deploy (`npx wrangler login`, then `npm run deploy`) or run `npm run serve` plus `cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8787`. Then host on one computer and join from another (for example a phone on mobile data) with the room code. Try closing the joiner's tab mid-game and reopening the link: they should return to their seat.
2. **Frame rate (item 15).** On a mid-range laptop, play a game at Normal and at Cinematic speed. If animation ever drops below ~40 fps, the game switches to the Performance preset and says so; that toast should not appear in normal play.
3. **A real touch device.** Play a few turns on a tablet in landscape: drag a unit onto the board, drag Equipment onto a unit, tap to attack.
