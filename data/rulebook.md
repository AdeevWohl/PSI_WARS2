# Overview

In the 37th century most of humanity has been wiped out. Competing warlords have harnessed the knowledge of Creation — Digital Splicing, Neurogenesis, Bio-Acceleration and Material Animation — and wage apocalyptic wars for planetary control. As a Galactic Warlord you build a Lab, create an army of Creatures, Robots and Cyborgs, and obliterate the Labs of rival warlords.

**Objective.** Reduce every opponent's Lab to 0 Hit Points (HP) or less. You also win when an opponent has no Battle Units left anywhere. Psi Wars is played by 2 players, or by 3–4 in free-for-all (see *3–4 players*).

"Cyber" and "Digital" mean the same ability (green). The game uses *Cyber*.

# Card types

Each player has a **Battle Deck** (blue backs) and a **Resource Deck** (gold backs).

**Battle Deck cards**

- **Battle Unit** — attacks and defends. Types: *Creature* (strong Psionic), *Robot* (strong Cyber) and *Cyborg* (both; can Stack). A *Commander* is a Battle Unit with an aura ability.
- **Equipment** — attached under one Battle Unit to raise one of its abilities.
- **Power** — a one-time effect, then discarded. **Traps** are a kind of Power card.

**Resource Deck cards**

- **Creation Units (CUs)** — the "mana" that pays for Battle Deck cards. Four types: Digital Splicing (green), Neurogenesis (blue), Bio-Acceleration (red), Material Animation (purple).
- **Creation Oracle** — searches your Resource Deck for a Creation Unit.

**Reading a card.** The column of five lights at the top left is the creation cost: green, blue, red and purple lights are the CUs of that type to *deplete*; the white light is the number of CUs to *destroy*. The name runs down the left edge. The three boxes at the bottom are the ability strengths — green Cyber, blue Psionic, red Physical (a value may be **X**, rolled each turn). Equipment shows the bonus it grants in those boxes. Small chips over a box show a value that differs from the printed one (Equipment, auras, X results, rebalanced cards).

# The dice

Each player has one die of each ability. Faces:

| Side | Cyber die (green) | Psionic die (blue) | Physical die (red) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cyber +1 and may re-roll your Physical die | Psionic +1 and may re-roll your Physical die | Physical +1 |
| 2 | Cyber +2 | Psionic +2 | Physical +2 |
| 3 | Cyber +3 | Psionic +3 | Physical +2 |
| 4 | Cyber +4 | Psionic +4 | Physical +3 |
| 5 | Cyber +1 and Physical +1 | Psionic +1 and Physical +1 | Physical +4 |
| 6 (Obliterate) | Cyber +1 and destroy the opponent's Cyber die | Psionic +1 and destroy the opponent's Psionic die | Physical +1 and destroy the opponent's Physical die |

**Numeric value** (used for X abilities, Dark Shredder, Grankor Stasis Field and the first-player roll): sides 1–5 count as 1–5, Obliterate counts as **4**. Even/odd tests use this value, so Obliterate is even. Unless a card says otherwise these rolls use the red die.

**Optional variant — no modifier dice:** each die simply adds +1 to +6, equal to its side, with no special effects. Choose it when setting up a game.

# Setup

1. Each player chooses a deck (Battle Deck + Resource Deck). Both are shuffled.
2. Choose starting Lab HP for everyone: 10 (short, ~30 min), 15 (default) or 20 (long, 45–60 min); any value 10–20 is allowed.
3. Each player draws **5 cards from their Battle Deck**. No Resource cards are drawn.
4. Everyone rolls the red die (Obliterate = 4); highest goes first; ties re-roll.
5. Turns pass in seat order. On the **first player's very first turn** they do not draw from the Battle Deck (they still draw a Resource card).

**RULING A5-a:** there is no mulligan and no maximum hand size.

# Turn structure

Every turn has five phases, always in this order:

1. **Replenish** — un-deplete all your Creation Units and Battle Units.
2. **Draw** — draw 1 Resource card (resolve it), then 1 Battle card.
3. **Creation** — pay costs to play Battle Units, Equipment and Power cards. Play as many as you can afford.
4. **Battle** — optionally attack with your ready Battle Units; opponents defend; battles resolve.
5. **Cleanup** — surviving attackers return to your Lab; stacks split; trapped units try to escape; victory is checked.

**Depleting.** Using a card depletes it (shown rotated 90° and greyed). Depleted cards are made ready again in their owner's Replenish phase.

# Draw phase

- Draw the top card of your Resource Deck. A **Creation Unit** goes straight into your Lab, ready to use this turn.
- A **Creation Oracle** gives you a choice: keep it in hand to use in a later Creation phase (free), or discard it now to search your Resource Deck for a Creation Unit of your choice, put it into your Lab ready, and shuffle.
- Then draw the top card of your Battle Deck (except on the first player's first turn).
- An empty deck simply means no draw. There is no penalty.

# Creation phase

**Paying a cost**

1. For each coloured light with value N, deplete N ready Creation Units of that type.
2. For the white light W, also destroy W Creation Units (to your discard pile). They must be ready, not among those you are depleting, and of a type that appears in the card's coloured cost.

**RULING A7-a:** a card with a white cost but no coloured cost (Equipment Hunter, Equipment Mechanic, Grankor Stasis Field) may destroy Creation Units of any type.

**RULING A7-b:** when there is a choice of which Creation Units to destroy, you choose. The game suggests the payment that keeps your most flexible Creation Units, and you can always pick another.

**RULING A7-c:** Creation Units are never played from hand; they enter the Lab in the Draw phase.

**When a card is created**

- **Battle Units enter play ready** and may attack the turn they are created (no "summoning sickness").
- **Equipment** is attached under one of your Battle Units. Each unit holds one Equipment. The unit must be a type the Equipment allows and have a value above 0 in the boosted ability (X counts). Equipment **works immediately** — on attack and on defence — and is **never depleted**. To give an equipped unit new Equipment, first **move** its old Equipment to another legal unit or **discard** it. Equipment can never exist on its own: if its unit leaves play, the Equipment goes to the discard pile. You may move or discard Equipment in your Creation phase and in your declaration step of a battle.
- **Power cards** take effect and are then discarded. You may play them in your own Creation phase and in any player's Battle step 3. **Traps** may be played at any time (see *Traps and responses*).

**RULING A7-d:** you may play Power cards during an opponent's Battle step 3 too, paying with your Creation Units that are still ready. Holding Creation Units back is a real strategy.

**RULING A7-e:** Equipment stays attached when its unit is depleted or replenished. If the unit leaves play, its Equipment goes to the owner's discard pile.

# Battle phase

The active player is the attacker. Steps, in order:

1. Attacker's declaration
2. Defenders' declaration
3. Power-card window
4. Cyborg abilities
5. Battle determination

**RULING A8-a:** Cyborg Abilities come before Battle Determination.

**Step 1 — Attacker's declaration.** You may first move Equipment between your units and form Stacks. Then choose any number of your ready, untrapped Battle Units (or Stacks) to attack. Attacking depletes them. You may choose not to attack. With several opponents, each attacker picks which opponent it attacks.

**Stacking.** A Stack is exactly two Battle Units fighting as one: one Cyborg and one non-Cyborg. Two Cyborgs cannot stack, and a stack never has more than two units. Each unit keeps its own Equipment. A Stack's strengths are the sum of everything in it. If a Stack is destroyed, everything in it is destroyed. Both units of an attacking Stack must be ready; defending Stacks may use depleted units. Stacks separate at the end of the Battle phase.

**Step 2 — Defenders' declaration.** Each attacked player may move Equipment and form Stacks, then assign defenders. Each defending unit or Stack blocks one attacker. Depleted units may defend; trapped units may not. Any attacker with no defender is unblocked.

**RULING A8-b:** at most one defender may block each attacker. You may leave any or all attackers unblocked.

**Step 3 — Power-card window.** Starting with the attacker, every player in turn order may play Power cards (including Traps), paying normally, until everyone passes in succession. This window **always happens**, even when nobody attacked. A unit removed from a battle (for example by a Trap or High-Energy Pulse) stops fighting: if it was an attacker, its defender does not fight; if it was a defender, its attacker becomes unblocked. If one unit of a Stack is trapped, the stack breaks and the other unit fights on alone.

**Step 4 — Cyborg abilities.** The attacker, then the defenders, use any optional Cyborg abilities that happen now (for example Lodner Predator's attack roll).

**Step 5 — Battle determination.** The attacker chooses the order of the blocked battles. Each is resolved completely through stages a–d; then all unblocked attackers deal damage (stage e).

- **a) X and modifiers.** Roll any X abilities (the die's numeric value; it lasts the rest of the turn) and any "may roll to modify" effects (decided before rolling). Then apply Equipment, Commander auras and Stack sums. *Which dice to roll:* a Cyber battle happens only if both sides have Cyber of at least 1; a Psionic battle only if both have Psionic of at least 1; a Physical battle always happens. (So Robots never fight Creatures psionically, and Creatures never fight Robots digitally.)
- **Obliteration.** For each die type, if exactly one side rolled Obliterate, the other side's die of that type is destroyed: its whole result (bonus, Physical +1, re-roll icon) is removed. The Obliterate face still gives its +1. If both rolled Obliterate, both dice are destroyed and neither gets any bonus.
- **b) Cyber battle.** Total = Cyber strength + surviving Cyber die. The lower total becomes **Disoriented**. Ties: nothing.
- **c) Psionic battle.** The same with Psionic. The higher total is the *Psionic win*.
- **d) Physical battle.** For each surviving re-roll icon a player *may* re-roll their Physical die once (the attacker decides first, then the defender; the new result replaces the old). Physical Obliteration is checked after all re-rolls. Total = Physical strength + surviving Physical die + 1 for each surviving Cyber/Psionic side 5. The higher total destroys the other side — unless the winner is Disoriented, in which case nothing is destroyed. A Disoriented unit still adds its full Physical total and can be destroyed. Ties, or both sides Disoriented: nothing is destroyed. Destroyed units go to their owner's discard pile with their Equipment and stack partners. Then "on win" and "on destroy" abilities resolve.
- **e) Unblocked attackers.** Each deals damage to the attacked player's Lab equal to its current Physical strength (base + Equipment + Stack + Commander + modifiers). No dice are rolled.

**RULING A8-c:** Disoriented lasts until the end of the current Battle phase and only matters in the battle where it happened.

**RULING A8-d:** a re-roll is always optional; the game shows both current totals and asks you.

# Cleanup phase

1. Your surviving attackers return to your Lab and are made ready. Stacks separate.
2. Each of your trapped units makes one escape attempt, following its Trap card.
3. Creation Units are not replenished now — that happens in your next Replenish phase.
4. Victory is checked; play passes to the next player.

**RULING A9-a:** escape attempts happen once per turn, in the trapped unit owner's Cleanup phase. A freed unit returns to its owner's Lab depleted; the Trap card goes to the discard pile of the player who played it.

# Traps and responses

Traps are **instants**: a Trap may be played on an opposing Battle Unit at any time, in any phase, by any player — whenever the game gives you a window. Windows open after a card is played, at the end of the Draw and Creation phases, after attackers are declared, in the Battle step 3 Power-card window, and before Cleanup escapes.

When a card goes on the stack, every player may respond with a Trap of their own. Responses resolve **last played, first resolved**. A player who plays a card keeps priority and may play another. When everyone passes in succession, the top card resolves. A Trap whose target is no longer a legal unit when it resolves has no effect.

A trapped unit is set aside: it cannot attack, defend, or use or grant abilities (including Commander auras). It keeps its Equipment. Only Traps are instant — other Power cards are played in your own Creation phase and in Battle step 3.

**Response windows online.** Every player gets the same short window (3 seconds by default), so a pause never reveals who holds a Trap. The optional *fast windows* setting skips players who cannot play anything — faster, but it leaks that information.

# Winning and losing

- A player loses as soon as their Lab is at 0 HP or less.
- A player loses as soon as they have no Battle Units in their Lab, hand and Battle Deck combined. Trapped units count as being in the Lab.
- This is checked after every damage event and at every phase change.

**RULING A10-a:** if everyone remaining would lose at the same moment, the game is a draw.

# 3–4 players

Free-for-all; last player standing wins.

- **Choosing targets:** each attacking unit picks which opponent it attacks — an army may be split.
- **Blocking:** only the player being attacked may block.
- **Power-card window:** every player may act, in turn order starting with the attacker, until everyone passes in succession.
- **Elimination:** an eliminated player's cards all leave the game; units they had trapped are freed and return to their owners depleted.
- **"Opponent" in card text** means the player on the other side of that battle: Blade Master's 1 damage hits the Lab of the player whose unit it destroyed; Lodner Predator mills the defender's Resource Deck. High-Energy Pulse affects everyone.
- **Starting HP and first turn:** the same starting HP as two players; only the first player skips their first Battle-Deck draw.

# Keywords

- **X ability** — rolled in Battle stage a (or when first needed this turn), using the red die unless stated: value 1–5, Obliterate = 4. X always counts as at least 1 for deciding which battles happen and for Equipment. Outside battle it shows as X.
- **Commander aura** — "All attacking [type]s get +A and +B": while the Commander is in play and not trapped, every attacking unit of that type its owner controls (including the Commander itself) gets the bonus. In a Stack the bonus counts once per qualifying unit; two Commanders' auras add up.
- **Psionic win** — this unit (or its stack) had the strictly higher total in the Psionic battle. *Cyber win* and *Physical win* work the same way.
- **Destroys a Battle Unit** — this unit (or its stack) won the Physical battle and destroyed the opposing unit or stack.
- **Search deck** — look through the named deck, take the card, then shuffle that deck. If none is found, still shuffle.
- **Reveal** — show the chosen card to the opponent; it stays in hand. With Jingwa Psi Avenger the opponent chooses which Power card to show; if they have none, they reveal their whole hand.
- **Discard pile** — destroyed and used cards go to their owner's discard pile. Discard piles are public (click one to look).

# Deck construction

- **Battle Deck:** 30–40 cards, at least 12 Battle Units, at most 3 copies of any card.
- **Resource Deck:** 18–24 cards, at most 20 Creation Units, at most 8 of any one Creation Unit type, at most 3 copies of any other card (such as the Creation Oracle).

The Deck Builder enforces all of these. Two starter decks are included: Creature Commander and Robot Commander.
